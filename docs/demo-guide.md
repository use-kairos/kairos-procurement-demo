# Demo guide (about 3 minutes)

Open the demo URL. The intro plays on its own, and Pip's conversation replays when the workspace opens.

**0:00 · Intro (auto).** "Agents will transact across company boundaries. Kairos is the transaction layer that turns an agent's intent into an approved, verifiable action. Every agent carries a passport."

**0:20 · Intent.** In the sidebar: "Alex runs a bakery with six agents. Pip buys stock, inside a passport Alex signed: up to SGD 1,000, known suppliers only."

**0:40 · Plan and agent to agent.** In Pip's thread: "Pip checks the cash reserve, then negotiates with the supplier's agent. Before the deal Kairos answers three questions: who is acting, under what authority, and is the money real?"

**1:10 · Approval.** Click **Run checks** in the Kairos view. "Six checks: registered agent, valid passport, the owner's key, inside the mandate, AML, payment rail."

**1:30 · Action.** On the payment card: "Kairos issues a single-use card locked to this order. Over the limit or a different merchant gets declined by the card itself. This is where Airwallex Issuing plugs in."

**1:50 · Outside the passport.** Open **Wren** → Run checks → **Pick up signing key** → OK. "Investing is outside Wren's passport, so it stops for Alex's key."

**2:20 · Refused.** Open **Sol**. "An agent with no passport asks for a refund to a new account. All three questions fail. Nothing moves."

**2:40 · Proof.** Point at the audit log. "Every decision is a chained receipt."

Close with: "Today this is a simulated concept. In the build phase we wire the card step to Airwallex Issuing sandbox."

Tips: `?skip` skips the intro. The **Guide** button (bottom left) reopens the walkthrough.
